About

Engineer by training.
Entrepreneur by instinct.
Operator by choice.

I grew up in a family of farmers and entrepreneurs in the Swiss countryside. My father and brothers were motorcyclists, so at 15 I became a motorcycle mechanic. I studied mechanical engineering and by 25 was designing CNC machines for the Swiss watch industry.

That is when I realized I was more drawn to building businesses than to pure engineering.

The Story

Twenty-five years. Three continents.

Age 15 — Switzerland

The mechanic who wanted to build businesses

I started as a motorcycle mechanic apprentice at 15, then studied mechanical engineering and spent my mid-twenties designing high-precision CNC machines for the Swiss watch industry: 2,000+ components, a team of four, serious budget responsibility. Technically rigorous work — but I kept noticing that the business decisions around the products interested me more than the products themselves.

2010 — California, US

The MBA that changed the plan

At 28, I moved to the US for an MBA from UC San Diego focused on entrepreneurship. I planned to launch my own company. Instead, a decade inside Johnson & Johnson and Philip Morris International taught me something more valuable: how large organizations actually make decisions and scale.

At PMI, I relocated to Singapore to lead the development and industrialization of IQOS. A team of 20-30 engineers growing to 50+. $100M+ in project budgets. 40+ suppliers across Asia. Production capacity of 50 million devices per year. IQOS now generates over $17B ARR.

2020 — Singapore

Building independently

In 2020, I joined MDS+, a 3-person engineering firm. One month later, COVID hit. We took a reusable face mask from idea to first sale in under 60 days: S$1.5M in sales, 20+ countries, certified Class I medical device. I deliberately expanded MDS+'s European client base and grew the business 8x, building the team, ISO 13485 QMS, and operational backbone that made it possible.

Beyond MDS+, I co-founded three hardware companies focused on commercialization: Gill Lab, InnoCity Lab, and MDS Safetech. All three were built from zero. I care as much about revenue as I do about engineering.

Today — Switzerland

Back where it started. With everything in between.

I am back in Switzerland — a move that reflects how MDS+ grew. I deliberately built our European client pipeline, and relocating was the natural next step: stay close to the clients I brought in, and put myself at the center of a region full of hardware and medtech startups I can support with 25+ years of product industrialization depth.

I mentor at ETH Zurich, invest in early-stage companies, and judge venture competitions.

Working Style

How I Work

01

I operate, not just advise

I have skin in the game. I run a team, build products, and manage P&L. When I work with clients, I draw on what I am doing right now — not what I did ten years ago.

02

I work best in ambiguous situations

I am naturally drawn to problems without obvious solutions. I build from first principles and move quickly once a direction is clear. Complexity does not slow me down.

03

I communicate directly

I say what I think, ask what I do not know, and give clear recommendations rather than lists of options. No slide decks that avoid the real question.

04

I build lasting relationships

Most of my work comes through my network. I invest in relationships before I need them — and I stay engaged long after a project ends.
